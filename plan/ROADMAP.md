# Slipfork Roadmap (`plan/ROADMAP.md`)

Owned by `strategist`. Workspace version `< 1.0.0` carries **zero backward-compatibility cruft**: one canonical way, immediate scorched-earth deletion of superseded designs, and mandatory human sign-off at architecture crossroads.

The clean-sheet contract lives in `specs/` (`01`–`06`) and root `TESTING_PLAN.md`. Everything before `M3.37` is history and is not rebuilt.

| **M2** | `—` (historical) | **Single-Origin `.localhost` Edge, `OSPP v1` Secrets & Live VCS Worlds (superseded)** | Delivered the initial daemon edge, `OSPP v1` exchange, and VCS workspaces; superseded by single-binary `M3`. |
