# Slipfork Roadmap (`plan/ROADMAP.md`)

Owned by `strategist`. Workspace version `< 1.0.0` carries **zero backward-compatibility cruft**: one canonical way, immediate scorched-earth deletion of superseded designs, and mandatory human sign-off at architecture crossroads.

The clean-sheet contract lives in `specs/` (numbered `01`–`06`) and root `TESTING_PLAN.md`. Everything before `M3.37` is history and is not rebuilt.
